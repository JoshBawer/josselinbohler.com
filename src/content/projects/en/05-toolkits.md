## The method is the product

Each skill states its inputs, checks, outputs, uncertainty and review gates. That makes it useful to an engineer and inspectable by another expert. The goal is not to automate judgement; it is to make the repeatable parts of the judgement process available on demand.

## Content with evidence behind it

The public tools also serve as working examples. A maturity score, FMECA guide or ROI model is more useful than another opinion about what AI might eventually do.
